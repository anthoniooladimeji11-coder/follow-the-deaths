Method

How the index is built, and what the labels mean.

The index in one sentence

The share of total funding that would have to move between pathogens for each one's funding share to equal its share of the burden. 0% is perfect alignment.

GMI = ½ Σ |funding share − burden share| (total-variation distance)

Labels

  • Neglected: under-funded, and a below-median share of late-stage projects.
  • Pipeline OK: under-funded, but well stocked with late-stage drugs.
  • Well-resourced: over-funded, with a strong pipeline.
  • Low translation: over-funded, but few late-stage drugs.

Vivli 2026 study data

Funding and pipeline: Global AMR R&D Hub (18,853 projects, USD 18.9B; USD 4.17B names one of the six). Burden: GRAM 2019, The Lancet 2022. 2030: Bayesian projection of extensively drug-resistant prevalence from 17.5 million Vivli AMR Register lab results.

Limits

Hub funding is tagged by genus, not species, and has no drug-class field. Burden is a 2019 global snapshot. Streptococcus funding includes vaccine and non-pneumoniae work.
